Date: Saturday, August 10, 2024 Location: Whiffletree Farm, Warrenton, VA Address: 8717 Springs Rd, Warrenton, VA 20186 Time: 7 PM - Midnight

Join DC Social Collective for an unforgettable evening at Whiffletree Farm in Warrenton, VA, where you can dance the night away under the stars. Enjoy live music by Marie Miller, country line dancing lessons, and classic American fare in a picturesque farm setting. It's the perfect night for mingling, socializing, and celebrating summer with friends old and new.

Volunteer Opportunity: We’re looking for enthusiastic volunteers to help set up the event, decorate, and handle registration. Volunteers will receive free admission to the event. If you're interested in volunteering, please reply to this email or contact us at info@dcsocialcollective.com .

We’re thrilled to announce that our friend, the talented singer/songwriter Marie Miller, will be stopping by to play her favorite country covers from 8:30 PM to 10:00 PM ! Marie, hailing from the Blue Ridge Mountains in Virginia, has an impressive musical career and will add an extra special touch to our evening. 🌟🎤
