Join DC Social Collective for an unforgettable evening at our Marina Mixer, a perfect blend of happy hour, dancing, and boat rides designed for young adults in their 20s and 30s. Set against the stunning backdrop of the Potomac River, this free event promises an evening of fun, relaxation, and socializing with fellow DC area young professionals.

Social Happy Hour: Kick off the evening by mingling with friends and meeting new people over delicious food and refreshing drinks.

Dancing & Music: Groove to the beats of our live DJ, ensuring the dance floor stays lively all night.

Exclusive Boat Rides: Our friend Andy Craig will be offering limited boat rides, providing a unique opportunity to explore the Potomac River. See our Nation’s Capital and monuments from the best vantage point in the city - the water.
