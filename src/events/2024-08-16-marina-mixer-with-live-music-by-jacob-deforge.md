Location: Columbia Island Marina, George Washington Memorial Pkwy, Arlington, VA, 22202, United States Time: 7:00 - 10:00 PM (Jacob DeForge will perform from 7:30 PM - 8:30 PM)

Join us for the 2nd edition of our Marina Mixer series , an unforgettable evening on the stunning Potomac waterfront, right in front of the Pentagon! This hidden gem of a venue offers the perfect backdrop for a summer evening of music, mingling, and merriment.

Enjoy live music by local Americana musician Jacob Deforge, who will be performing from 7:30 PM to 8:30 PM. His soulful tunes are sure to set the perfect vibe for a relaxed and enjoyable evening.

Beautiful Waterfront Venue: Experience the picturesque views of the Potomac River as the sun sets over the water.
