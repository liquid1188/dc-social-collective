Presented in collaboration with DC Social Collective 📅 Saturday, May 17 & Sunday, May 18 🕐 1:00 PM – 5:00 PM 📍 President Lincoln’s Cottage 140 Rock Creek Church Road NW, Washington, DC 20011

DC Social Collective is thrilled to partner with President Lincoln’s Cottage for the 10th Annual Bourbon & Bluegrass Festival , a weekend of live music, southern flavor, and meaningful celebration on the historic grounds where President Lincoln drafted the Emancipation Proclamation.

Come solo or with friends and enjoy an afternoon lounging on Lincoln’s lawn with bourbon in hand, bluegrass in the air, and the company of fellow young professionals and history lovers.

All proceeds benefit the preservation and educational mission of President Lincoln’s Cottage , a 501(c)(3) nonprofit and National Monument.
