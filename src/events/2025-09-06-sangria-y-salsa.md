DC Social Collective and The Winery at Bull Run are throwing another BANGER that will sell out ,  spicy and sizzling like that plate of fajitas passing your table where everyone’s like, “Oooooh I’ll take whatever they’re having.” Yeah, that.

We’re talking salsa under the stars, empanadas in one hand, wine in the other, and over 400+ of DC’s finest young professionals dancing across a full vineyard campus all night long.

WHEN + WHERE Saturday, September 6 7:30 PM – 11:00 PM The Winery at Bull Run 15950 Lee Hwy, Centreville, VA

No partner? No experience? No problem. We’re kicking things off with a laid-back salsa lesson from Jude Moukarzel and Tara Vides of Inspira Dance. It’s low-pressure, high energy, and made to get everyone out on the floor.
