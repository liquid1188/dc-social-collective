Date: Saturday, October 5th Time: 7:00 PM - 12:00 AM (live music from 8:30 PM - 10:30 PM) Location: Whiffletree Farm, 8717 Springs Road, Warrenton, VA

Description: Embrace the autumn charm at our "Harvest Moon Barn Dance" at Whiffletree Farm. Inspired by Neil Young's music, this event offers a chance to connect with the community through live music and dancing. Arrive to the warm glow of twinkling lights and cozy hay bale seating, creating a relaxed atmosphere perfect for mingling with friends and enjoying the evening’s vibrant sounds and rhythms.

Live Music & Dancing: Enjoy dancing to a live band from 8:30 PM - 10:00 PM, with plenty of opportunities to get on your feet and move to the music.

Country Dance Lesson by Inspira Dance: Participate in a country dance lesson from 7:30 PM - 8:30 PM, learning new moves from the experts at Inspira Dance.
