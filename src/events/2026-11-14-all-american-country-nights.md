Country swing, 2-step, line dancing, live country music, BBQ, and wine is coming to the historic grounds of Blue Valley Vineyard & Winery for another massive fall country night under the lights.

After nonstop demand, DC Social Collective is officially running it back month after month with All-American Country Nights, our ongoing country dance series bringing together hundreds from across DC, Northern Virginia, and Maryland.

Expect beginner-friendly lessons, a live DJ, open dancing all night, and the kind of crowd that actually comes ready to have fun, meet people, and dance. Whether you’re brand new to country swing or already know your way around the floor, this is the DMV country series everyone’s talking about.

Grab your boots, bring your friends, and get ready for another unforgettable night at Blue Valley.
