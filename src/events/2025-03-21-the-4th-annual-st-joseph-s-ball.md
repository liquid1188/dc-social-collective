Gather at the prestigious "St. Joseph's Ball," hosted in the Father O'Connell Hall at The Catholic University of America. Celebrate the betrothal of Mary and St. Joseph in an evening filled with elegance and connection. This gala, perfect for both singles and couples, serves as an excellent opportunity to forge new relationships within the Catholic community.

Enjoy a variety of light hors d'oeuvres and a selection of beverages from our cash bar, carefully chosen to complement the evening's sophisticated atmosphere.

Kick off the night with a waltz lesson from 7:30 PM to 8:30 PM, open to dancers of all skill levels. After mastering the basics, join fellow attendees on the dance floor to live music that will enchant and entertain throughout the night.

The venue, known for its stunning marble floors and elegant architecture, provides the ideal backdrop for an unforgettable night. Designed to reflect both dignity and charm, the ballroom ensures every moment is filled with grace and beauty.
