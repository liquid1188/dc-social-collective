Fall nights hit different under a harvest moon.

DC Social Collective is back at Whiffletree Farm for a barn-burning autumn throwdown that’ll have you two-stepping till midnight. Think boots kicking up dust, sparks flying off the bonfire, and the kind of country swing that makes strangers instant dance partners.

These barn dances are some of our most popular events, drawing hundreds of young adults from Virginia, Maryland, DC, and beyond every year. If you’ve been, you already know. If you haven’t, this is the one to circle on your calendar.

We’re talking Harvest Moon Hoedown , a night of country swing, 2-step, line dancing, and boot-stomping beginner lessons that’ll keep the floor packed from the first beat to the last.
