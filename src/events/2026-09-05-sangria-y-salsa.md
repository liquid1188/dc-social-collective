Salsa, bachata, live Latin music, sangria, and bold flavors, set against the entire historic vineyard grounds and campus of The Winery at Bull Run.

DC Social Collective and The Winery at Bull Run invite you to the return of Sangria & Salsa, the DMV's largest bimonthly Latin dance series bringing 600+ young professionals together from across DC, Northern Virginia, and Maryland.

This is one of our most electric event formats for a reason: welcoming beginner lessons, nonstop dancing, incredible and spicy live music, and a crowd that shows up ready to connect and move.
