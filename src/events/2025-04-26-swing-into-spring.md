Escape the city for a night and embrace the rustic charm of Whiffletree Farm with our "Swing into Spring Barn Dance." It's an ideal blend of fantastic music, dance lessons, and a vibrant young adult social atmosphere that sets this event apart from any other!

Swing Dance Lessons: Kick off the evening from 7:30 PM to 8:30 PM with beginner-friendly lessons in East Coast Swing and Lindy Hop. Perfect for newcomers eager to learn, as well as seasoned dancers looking to polish their moves. No partner? No worries! Feel free to come solo, bring a partner, or gather a group of friends for a fantastic start to the night.

Live DJ: Post-lessons, our DJ will take the stage, spinning a seamless mix of swing classics and modern hits to keep you dancing till midnight.

Social Dancing All Night: After the structured lessons, the floor is yours for freestyle social dancing. Enjoy practicing your new moves, mingling with other guests, and dancing through the night in a spirited and welcoming environment.
