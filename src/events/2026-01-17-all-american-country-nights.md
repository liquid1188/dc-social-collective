Country swing, 2-step, line dancing, live country music, and BBQ + wine, set against the entire historic vineyard grounds and campus of The Winery at Bull Run .

DC Social Collective and The Winery at Bull Run invite you to the launch of All-American Country Nights , a new bimonthly country dance series bringing hundreds of young professionals together from across DC, Northern Virginia, and Maryland.

This is one of our most widely attended event styles for a reason: welcoming lessons, high-energy dancing, incredible live music, and a crowd that shows up ready to meet people and move.
