Step into a romantic evening at the "Love Never Sinks: A Valentine’s Day Ball," hosted in the breathtaking Heritage Hall Ballroom at Hotel Heron. This exclusive event blends the timeless charm of Alexandria with a touch of Titanic-inspired elegance, offering a night of connection, dancing, and celebration for singles and couples alike.

Surround yourself with DC's vibrant community of young professionals as the evening unfolds. Enjoy the rhythm of a live 1920s-inspired band and immerse yourself in an atmosphere of romance and nostalgia, ensuring your Valentine’s Day weekend is unforgettable.

This is more than an event, it’s your chance to step inside a story, where timeless romance and elegance come alive, creating an unforgettable evening inspired by the grandeur of the Titanic.

Begin your evening with a beginner’s dance lesson , perfect for all skill levels. Whether it’s your first time or you’re a seasoned dancer, you’ll feel right at home as you learn steps to classic ballroom dances. Following the lesson, the floor will open for everyone to showcase their moves to the sounds of a live 1920s-inspired band that will fill the room with rhythm and excitement.
