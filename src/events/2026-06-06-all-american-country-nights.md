Country swing, 2-step, line dancing, live country music, BBQ, and wine return to the historic grounds of The Winery at Bull Run for another massive summer country night under the lights.

After a packed-out launch and nonstop demand, DC Social Collective is officially running it back month after month with All-American Country Nights, our ongoing country dance series bringing together hundreds from across DC, Northern Virginia, and Maryland.

Expect beginner-friendly lessons, a live country band and DJ, open dancing all night, and the kind of crowd that actually comes ready to have fun, meet people, and dance. Whether you’re brand new to country swing or already know your way around the floor, this is the summer series everyone’s talking about.

Grab your boots, bring your friends, and get ready for another unforgettable night at Bull Run.
