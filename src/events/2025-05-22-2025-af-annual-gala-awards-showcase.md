Grab your flip phones, Lipsmackers, and disposable cameras! The America’s Future Annual Gala and Awards Showcase is an ode to our founding year – 1995!

DC Social Collective is proud to serve as a Host Committee Sponsor for this milestone celebration. Join us on the actual anniversary, May 22, 2025 , at AutoShop at Union Market in Washington, DC, for a fantastic evening of fun, friends, fancy dress, and the induction of the newest members of the 1995 Society .

For more than two decades, the AF Annual Gala has brought together civic-minded change-makers and local leaders from across the country to celebrate the past and look ahead to another exciting year.

Dress your best for the red carpet and photo booth, enjoy the open bar (including a VIP bar for special guests), mingle on the patio, relive your youth with some 90’s arcade games, and before the night is over ,  kick off your shoes and hit the dance floor!
