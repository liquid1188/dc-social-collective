Join us for our 3rd Annual St. Joseph's Ball!

St. Joseph's Ball is an annual formal event in celebration for St. Joseph's Feast Day to promote chastity, purity, and true romance as modeled in the betrothal of St. Joseph and Mary. The event is in collaboration with our friends and partners at Inspira Dance .

A 50% discount is available for all graduate and undergraduate CUA students . To claim your 50% coupon code, please email a screenshot of your CUA student ID to info@dcsocialcollective.com .

Starting Early is a rock trio made up of brothers Rory and Declan O'Donnell on guitars, along with Paul Mosimann on percussion.
