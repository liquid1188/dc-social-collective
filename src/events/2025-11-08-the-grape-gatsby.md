DC Social Collective and Old Farm Winery are uncorking an evening of elegance where velvet vocals, big band swing, and fine Virginia wine collide. Think jazz-age sparkle under the stars, string lights over the vines, and hundreds of DC’s most stylish young professionals stepping into a scene Fitzgerald himself would envy.

Saturday, November 8 7:00 PM – 11:00 PM Old Farm Winery at Hartland 23583 Fleetwood Rd, Aldie, VA

Set on 35 acres of preserved 18th-century farmland, Old Farm Winery blends rustic charm with timeless elegance. The renovated farmhouse, historic barn, outdoor bar, and expansive patio areas create the perfect backdrop for a Gatsby-inspired vineyard celebration.

7:00 PM ,  Social Happy Hour with the Scott Ragsdale Orchestra The evening begins with a happy hour reception while the Scott Ragsdale Orchestra , a society dance orchestra featuring former premier U.S. military band musicians. From the swinging sounds of Sinatra to Motown and modern dance classics, they’ll set the perfect Gatsby tone the moment the doors open.
